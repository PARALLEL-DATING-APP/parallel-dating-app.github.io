Delete your Parallel account

For the Parallel dating app. Effective 5 October 2026

You can delete your Parallel account and the data tied to it at any time. Deleting an account cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Parallel and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

What we delete

  • your profile and photos
  • your messages, matches and likes
  • your date requests and notifications
  • the ratings you gave and received, and your block list
  • the reports you sent, and your push notification token

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Reports other users filed about your account, for 12 months, to keep Parallel safe.
  • Backups, which may still contain your data until they expire after 7 days.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep.
