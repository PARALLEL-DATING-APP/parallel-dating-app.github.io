Who runs Parallel

Parallel is run by Pieter Van Der Berg, which decides why and how your personal data is used (the "controller"). Address: Hemvangen 13 Overtornea 95713. Contact: mvanderberg190@gmail.com.

You must be at least 18 to use Parallel. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete an account if we learn that its owner is under 18.

How we use it

  • Run your account and the dating features, such as profiles, matching, compatibility scores, date requests and chat. We need this data to provide Parallel to you.
  • Show nearby venues, events and films, using your location. We rely on your consent, and you can switch off location access in your phone's settings.
  • Send the notifications you ask for, such as likes, matches and date requests. You can switch each type off in Settings.
  • Keep Parallel safe: handle reports and blocks, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our rules. We have a legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • Meet legal duties and answer your requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and Parallel shows no advertising.

Who receives it

Other users see your name, photos, bio, age range, city, country, languages, gender, profile answers (including alcohol choices) and, if you chose to add them, your sexual orientation and your religion. Who you are interested in is never shown to other users. They also see roughly how far away you are, in 5 km steps. Only the person you are chatting with sees your chat messages.

Where it is stored

Our database, server and photos are stored with Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden (EU). Some providers above may process data outside the EU or EEA, for example in the United States. Where they do, we choose providers that use safeguards recognised under EU law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is active: we keep your account data.
  • When you delete your account: we delete your profile, photos, messages, matches, likes, date requests, notifications, ratings, block list, the reports you sent, and your push token.
  • Reports about you: we may keep reports other users filed about your account for 12 months to keep the service safe.
  • Backups: backups may still contain deleted data until they expire, after 7 days.
  • Server logs: we keep them for up to 7 days.
  • Legal duties: we keep anything the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (access and portability)
  • correct your data. You can edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • delete your data. Use Settings, Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account.
  • restrict or object to some uses of your data
  • withdraw your consent, for example for location, religion or sexual orientation, without affecting what we did before

Email mvanderberg190@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se).

Security

We protect your data with measures such as hashed passwords, access controls and an encrypted database. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Safety and reporting

You can block and report profiles. We review reports and may remove content or accounts that break our Community Standards.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters. Questions about this policy: mvanderberg190@gmail.com.
